Head atlas (assets/head_atlas.npz) for the NeuroMPR PACS scalp & skull layers

Derived from the CT head template (scct_unsmooth.nii, an average of real head CT scans) and
the matching skull-stripped T1 template (betsct1_unsmooth.nii) of the Clinical Toolbox:
  Rorden C, Bonilha L, Fridriksson J, Bender B, Karnath HO. Age-specific CT and MRI templates
  for spatial normalization. NeuroImage 2012;61(4):957-965.
  https://github.com/neurolabusc/Clinical
The surfaces were extracted with tools/build_head_atlas.py. Original licence:
